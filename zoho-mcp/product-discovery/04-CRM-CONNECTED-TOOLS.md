# CRM connected tools

These are the tools a CRM user touches during the day and how each one links back to the CRM. The first table shows connections Zoho CRM already has. The second section lists the gaps that are good product ideas. For the full list of daily tools, see [03-DAILY-WORK-TOOLS.md](03-DAILY-WORK-TOOLS.md).

---

## Already connected to Zoho CRM

| Daily tool | Connection | What it does for the CRM user |
|---|---|---|
| **Phone / VoIP** | PhoneBridge (Zoho Voice, RingCentral, Twilio, etc.) | Click to call, caller ID shows the contact's record, calls are logged automatically |
| **WhatsApp / SMS** | WhatsApp Business, SMS extensions | Message customers from the record and keep the history on it |
| **Video meetings** | Zoho Meeting, Zoom, Teams | Schedule from a deal, and attendance is logged |
| **Browser** | Zoho CRM browser extension, LinkedIn Sales Navigator | Save a lead from LinkedIn or a website without retyping |
| **Mobile** | Zoho CRM mobile app, Card Scanner | Check in at visits, scan business cards into leads |
| **Website chat** | Zoho SalesIQ | Visitor chats become leads, and the CRM shows the visitor's history |
| **Web forms** | Zoho Forms, CRM webforms | Form submissions create leads directly |
| **Booking links** | Zoho Bookings, Calendly | Booked meetings create CRM events |
| **Documents** | Zoho Writer mail merge, WorkDrive | Generate quotes and letters from record data, store files with the deal |
| **E-signature** | Zoho Sign, DocuSign | Send a contract from a deal and track the signature status |
| **Invoicing** | Zoho Books, Invoice, QuickBooks | Turn a won deal into an invoice and show payment status on the record |
| **Support** | Zoho Desk | Sales sees open tickets before calling a customer |
| **Marketing** | Zoho Campaigns, Marketing Automation | Email opens and clicks show up on the lead and feed lead scores |
| **Social** | Zoho Social | Social interactions linked to leads |
| **Team chat** | Zoho Cliq, Slack, Teams | Deal alerts, approvals and CRM lookups from chat |
| **Projects** | Zoho Projects | A won deal starts a delivery project |
| **Reports** | Zoho Analytics | CRM data combined with finance and support data |
| **Surveys** | Zoho Survey | Customer feedback (NPS) recorded on the account |
| **Events** | Zoho Backstage | Event registrants become leads |
| **Automation** | Zoho Flow, Zapier, Make | Glue for anything not covered above |

## Gaps that suggest product ideas

These connections are weak, missing, or done badly in many setups:

- **Meeting notes → CRM:** recording, transcript, summary and next steps written to the deal automatically.
- **Email → CRM fields:** reading a reply and updating the deal stage, amount or close date, not just logging the email.
- **WhatsApp voice notes:** transcribing them and attaching a summary to the record.
- **Shared inbox / support email → sales:** spotting upsell signals in support conversations.
- **Spreadsheets:** the Excel file salespeople keep on the side. Two-way sync or a clean import with duplicate checks.
- **Personal to-do apps:** CRM tasks are rarely in the same place as personal to-dos.
- **Field visits:** route planning, offline notes and photos that sync back to the record later.
- **Pre-call brief:** one view of a customer's emails, tickets, invoices and last meeting, five minutes before a call.
- **Approvals in chat:** approving a discount or quote in Cliq or Slack without opening the CRM.
- **Receipts and expenses:** linking client-visit expenses to the account they were for.

Each gap comes from a person copying information by hand from one tool to another. That's the workaround signal from [02-GENERATING-IDEAS-FOR-ANY-USER.md](02-GENERATING-IDEAS-FOR-ANY-USER.md).
