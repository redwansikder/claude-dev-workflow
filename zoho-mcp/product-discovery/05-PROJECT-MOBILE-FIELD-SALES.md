# Project: mobile field sales

Product ideas for CRM users who work on their phones: field reps, people at events, and visit-based sales teams. They build on Zoho Card Scanner and the Zoho CRM mobile app. For how these apps fit with other daily tools, see [04-CRM-CONNECTED-TOOLS.md](04-CRM-CONNECTED-TOOLS.md).

---

## Starting point: Zoho Card Scanner

Card Scanner is a separate app from the CRM mobile app. It can:

- Scan the front and back of business cards
- Read text in many languages (OCR) and fill in name, company, title, phone, email, address and website
- Bulk scan a stack of cards (after an event, for example)
- Export to **Zoho CRM or Bigin** as a lead or contact
- Export to phone contacts, Excel/CSV or vCard
- Add notes, tags and the event or location where you met
- Share your own digital business card by QR code
- Sync across devices

Features vary by platform and plan, so check the current Zoho docs before relying on one.

---

## Gaps that make good product ideas

- **Visit summary:** after a check-out, the rep speaks for 30 seconds, and AI writes a structured visit note and next steps onto the deal.
- **Card scan + enrichment:** after the scan, find the company's website and size, and check for duplicates against existing accounts before creating the lead.
- **Event mode:** scan 50 cards, tag them to one event, and send each person a personal follow-up email the next morning.
- **Offline photo evidence:** shelf or site photos tagged to the account with GPS and time, for audit or merchandising teams.
- **Daily route digest:** each morning, a list of the customers worth visiting near today's meetings, ranked by deal value or time since last contact.
