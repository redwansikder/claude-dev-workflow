# Daily work tools

Beyond email, calendar and chat, most office workers use tools in these groups every day. The Zoho product for each is in brackets.

---

## Communication

- **Video meetings:** Zoom, Google Meet, Teams (Zoho Meeting)
- **Phone and VoIP:** calls, voicemail, call logs (Zoho Voice, PhoneBridge)
- **Team feed and intranet:** announcements, forums (Zoho Connect)

## Work tracking

- **Tasks and to-dos:** personal lists, reminders (Zoho ToDo)
- **Project management:** boards, Gantt charts, sprints (Zoho Projects, Sprints)
- **Help desk and tickets:** customer support queues (Zoho Desk)
- **CRM:** leads, deals, follow-ups (Zoho CRM, Bigin)

## Documents and knowledge

- **Word processing:** Docs, Word (Zoho Writer)
- **Spreadsheets:** Excel, Sheets (Zoho Sheet)
- **Slides:** (Zoho Show)
- **File storage and sharing:** Drive, Dropbox, SharePoint (Zoho WorkDrive)
- **Notes and wiki:** Notion, OneNote, Confluence (Zoho Notebook, Learn)
- **E-signature:** DocuSign (Zoho Sign)
- **PDF tools:** viewing, editing, merging (Zoho PDF Editor)

## Money and operations

- **Invoicing and accounting:** (Zoho Books, Invoice)
- **Expenses and receipts:** (Zoho Expense)
- **Inventory and orders:** (Zoho Inventory)
- **Payroll:** (Zoho Payroll)

## People and HR

- **Time tracking and attendance:** clock-in, timesheets (Zoho People)
- **Leave requests and approvals:** (Zoho People)
- **Hiring:** (Zoho Recruit)

## Data and automation

- **Forms and surveys:** (Zoho Forms, Survey)
- **Reports and dashboards:** (Zoho Analytics)
- **Automation between apps:** Zapier, Make (Zoho Flow)
- **Small custom apps:** (Zoho Creator)

## Personal utilities

- **Password manager:** (Zoho Vault)
- **Bookmarks and read-later lists**
- **Screen capture and screen recording:** Loom, Snagit
- **Scheduling links:** Calendly (Zoho Bookings)
- **Search across everything:** finding a file or message is a daily pain in its own right

## Field and mobile

- **Business card scanner:** (Zoho Card Scanner)
- **Maps and route planning:** for sales visits (Zoho RouteIQ)
- **Mobile check-ins:** photos, GPS logging

---

## Where the ideas are

Look hardest at the gaps between these tools: copying data from email into the CRM, exporting spreadsheets to make reports, chasing approvals in chat. People build workarounds at those handoffs, which is the signal [02-GENERATING-IDEAS-FOR-ANY-USER.md](02-GENERATING-IDEAS-FOR-ANY-USER.md) tells you to look for.
