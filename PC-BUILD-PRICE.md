## Before buying

- **The processor is hard to find.** Star Tech lists the 4350G as discontinued, and many shops show it out of stock. Ask the shop for a current alternative for the same motherboard.
- **RAM and SSD prices are high right now.** Some shops list them for much less (e.g. ৳3,799 for the RAM at [Sumash Tech](https://www.sumashtech.com/product/corsair-vengeance-lpx-8gb-ddr4-3200mhz-heatsink-desktop-ram)), but check whether those are still in stock.
- **Ask about cash discounts and EMI.** Several shops give a lower price for cash.

## Notes on the current PC

1. **RAM runs at 2133MHz instead of 3200MHz.** Free fix: in the BIOS (press Del at startup), turn on **XMP**. This also makes the built-in graphics faster.
2. **8GB is low for development.** VS Code, a browser and a dev server together fill it quickly. Adding a second 8GB stick (about ৳7,500) gives 16GB, and two sticks also run faster than one.
