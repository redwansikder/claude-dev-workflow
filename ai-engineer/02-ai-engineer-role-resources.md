# AI Engineer Role: Videos and Blog Posts

These are about the AI Engineer job itself: what the role is, why it exists, and what the work looks like in practice. For learning resources by topic, see [05-ai-engineer-resources.md](05-ai-engineer-resources.md).

## YouTube / video

1. **swyx, "The 1,000x AI Engineer"** (AI Engineer Summit 2023 keynote)
   The talk that made the title popular. It explains what an AI Engineer is, how the role differs from an ML Engineer, and why software engineers are well placed to move into it.
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qaJXBMwUkoE

2. **"The Rise of AI Engineering – Making an Industry" with swyx** (Scrimba, ~37 min)
   How the role formed and what companies hire AI Engineers for. It's from a podcast for web developers.
   https://podcast.scrimba.com/146

3. **The Pragmatic Engineer, "AI Engineering with Chip Huyen"** (Feb 2025)
   Gergely Orosz interviews the author of the *AI Engineering* book. They cover how AI Engineering differs from ML Engineering, and what teams actually build and get wrong.
   https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/ai-engineering-with-chip-huyen

4. **Andrej Karpathy, "Software Is Changing (Again)"** (YC, June 2025)
   The big picture behind the role. Prompts are becoming a new kind of programming ("Software 3.0"), and that's why companies now need engineers who build with LLMs.
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LCEmiRjPEtQ

## Blog posts (with real-world cases)

1. **swyx, "The Rise of the AI Engineer"** (Latent Space, June 2023)
   The essay that defined the role. Work that used to take a research team years can now be built from API docs. It also draws the line between AI Engineer and ML Engineer.
   https://www.latent.space/p/ai-engineer

2. **AI Hero (Matt Pocock), "What is an AI Engineer?"**
   Written for TypeScript and web developers. It covers what the role means for a dev moving into AI.
   https://www.aihero.dev/what-is-an-ai-engineer

3. **CloudThat, "What Does an AI Engineer Actually Do All Day?"**
   A real-world look based on an analysis of 1,000+ job postings. Only 6.4% mention model training. Most of the work is data pipelines, evaluation, integration and deployment.
   https://www.cloudthat.com/resources/blog/what-does-an-ai-engineer-actually-do-all-day

4. **LinkedIn Engineering, "Musings on Building a Generative AI Product"**
   A real team's AI Engineer work on a shipped product: RAG, evals and prompt iteration. They got 80% of the way in the first month, and the last 20% took four more months.
   https://www.linkedin.com/blog/engineering/generative-ai/musings-on-building-a-generative-ai-product
